Getting-started checklist

Tips for irregular income

  • Budget against your lowest realistic month, not your best one — treat anything above that as a bonus to save or invest.
  • Split every client payment on arrival: a fixed % to savings first, the rest to spending. Automate the split mentally so it's not a decision each time.
  • Keep student costs and studio costs in separate mental (or literal) buckets — it makes it obvious which side needs attention.
  • A small, boring emergency fund beats a big exciting investment when income is inconsistent. Stability first, growth second.
  • Review subscriptions every semester — tools you needed for one project quietly become permanent costs.
