Stage 1 of 7 · Est. 3–5 hours

🎯 Strategy & Positioning

Before posting or reaching out to anyone, define exactly who you help, what problem you solve, and why someone should choose you.

Objectives

🎯Define your niche and ideal client profile (ICP) with laser precision
💡Craft a unique value proposition (UVP) that differentiates you
📣Create a one-liner pitch you can use across DMs, emails, and bios
🗺️Map your service offerings to specific client pain points
💰Set pricing strategy aligned with your target market

Action Tasks

Write down your top 3 skills and the specific outcomes you deliver (not just what you do)
Identify your niche: Industry + Role + Problem (e.g. "I help e-commerce brands reduce cart abandonment")
Write your Ideal Client Profile: describe their industry, size, budget, pain points, goals
Draft 3 variations of your one-liner pitch (for DM, email subject line, and bio)
List 5 specific pain points your ideal client faces daily
Define your service packages and pricing tiers (starter / standard / premium)
Research 3 competitors — note what they do well and where the gap is

AI Prompt Examples

🎯 Niche Definition Prompt

Help me identify 5 specific niche markets where these skills are highly valuable, and for each one: (1) describe the ideal client, (2) the pain point I solve, (3) a compelling one-liner pitch, (4) estimated budget range these clients typically have.
💡 UVP Generator Prompt
Create 5 unique value propositions for a freelance [SERVICE] who specializes in helping [TARGET CLIENT] achieve [OUTCOME]. Each UVP should: be under 15 words, focus on the result not the process, avoid generic terms like "high quality" or "experienced", and be suitable for an Instagram bio or cold DM opener.

Best Practices & Common Mistakes

✅ Best Practices
✓Niche down — "I help everyone" wins no one
✓Position around outcomes, not deliverables
✓Speak your client's language, not industry jargon
✓Test 2–3 positioning angles before committing
✓Price based on value delivered, not hours spent
❌ Common Mistakes
✗Listing skills instead of outcomes in your pitch
✗Targeting too broad an audience (everyone is no one)
✗Skipping this stage and going straight to posting
✗Copying competitors' positioning exactly
✗Underpricing to "start getting clients"

✓First line must be about THEM, not you
✓One CTA per email — one action only
✓Plain text emails outperform HTML templates for cold outreach
✓Send between 7am–10am in recipient's timezone
✓Never send more than 30 emails/day per inbox when starting
❌ Cold Email Killers
✗"I hope this email finds you well" — delete instantly
✗Long emails — keep it under 100 words
✗Talking about yourself in email 1
✗Sending from a new/unwarmed domain
✗No follow-up (80% of deals happen after follow-up 2–3)
✗Using unverified email lists

📞 Discovery Call Framework (5 Questions)
Ask these 5 questions before presenting anything: 1. "What's the #1 challenge you're facing with [RELEVANT AREA] right now?" 2. "How long has this been a problem, and what have you tried so far?" 3. "What would solving this mean for your business in the next 6 months?" 4. "What's stopping you from solving it yourself / with your current team?" 5. "If we could fix this, what does success look like to you?" Then: "Based on what you've shared, here's exactly how I'd approach this for you..."
📄 Proposal Generator Prompt
Write a one-page freelance proposal for a client who: is a [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY TYPE], their main problem is [PAIN POINT], they want to achieve [GOAL], and their budget range is [BUDGET]. My proposed solution is [YOUR APPROACH]. Include: Executive Summary, The Problem, My Proposed Solution (3 steps), What's Included, Investment, Timeline, and Next Steps. Keep it concise, confident, and results-focused. No fluff.
💬 Post-Call Follow-Up Email
Write a post-discovery-call follow-up email for a potential client. The call covered: [THEIR PAIN POINTS], [WHAT I PROPOSED], [AGREED NEXT STEP]. The email should: (1) open by referencing 1 specific thing they said, (2) recap the core problem and my solution in 2 sentences, (3) attach/link the proposal, (4) give a clear deadline ("I can hold this spot until [DATE]"), (5) end with an easy yes/no question. Under 120 words.

Additional AI Prompts

🔥 Monthly Review Prompt
I am a freelance [SERVICE] who has been doing social media outreach for [X weeks]. Here are my weekly stats: DMs sent: [X], Replies: [X], Calls booked: [X], Clients closed: [X]. My current content engagement is [LOW/MED/HIGH]. Analyze my conversion funnel, identify the weakest link, and give me 3 specific actions to take this week to improve my results.
📊 Testimonial Request Prompt
Write a casual WhatsApp/DM message asking a happy client for a testimonial. It should feel personal not scripted, remind them of the specific result we achieved together ([RESULT]), and give them 3 short questions to answer if they don't know what to write: (1) What was the situation before working with me? (2) What result did we achieve? (3) Who would you recommend me to?
🤝 Referral Request Prompt
Write a message to a past or current client asking for referrals. It should: feel natural and grateful, not transactional, remind them of the result we achieved, describe in 1 sentence exactly who my ideal referral is, and offer a small incentive (discount on next project / finder's fee). Keep it under 80 words.
