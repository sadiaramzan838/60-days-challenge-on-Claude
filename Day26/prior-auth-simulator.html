🧍 Patient Lane

1. Scenario Selected

Patient identifies a health need requiring care.

2. Schedule Provider Visit

Patient books an appointment with their provider.

🩺 Provider Lane

3. Medical Necessity Evaluation

Provider documents clinical justification for treatment.

4. Collect PA Documents

Required forms, notes, and records are gathered.

5. Submit to Payer

Completed PA request is sent to the insurance payer.

Appeal Submission

Provider/patient formally contests a denial.

Peer-to-Peer Review

Provider's physician discusses case directly with payer's medical director.

🏦 Payer Lane

6. Payer Review

Utilization management nurse/physician reviews the request.

7. Determination

Payer issues Approval, Pend, or Denial.

Final Determination

Outcome after appeal / peer-to-peer review.

8. Case Closed

Workflow finished — outcome delivered to patient and provider.
