Executive Summary

Q2 revenue grew 12%, short of the 18% annual target. The gap traces mainly to enterprise sales — of 14 large contracts expected to close, only 9 were finalized, due to procurement delays and additional security reviews requested by two clients. Marketing traffic rose 34% quarter-over-quarter, but conversion is still below expectations, prompting a planned website redesign and a simplified demo-request flow. Customer retention held strong at 94%, though support ticket volume rose significantly, driven largely by complaints about reporting performance and dashboard loading times — an issue also surfacing in sales conversations with prospects, and now treated as a priority. With engineering near capacity ahead of the August analytics dashboard release, leadership held off on approving new spending, public release commitments, hiring decisions, and the conference budget pending more information.
